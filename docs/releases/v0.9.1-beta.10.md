# v0.9.1-beta.10: bounded VOD startup and shared store ownership

Native VOD routes distinguish malformed selectors (400), missing targets (404),
unsupported playback modes (422), and upstream failures (502). The existing
startup timeout now covers descriptor resolution, waits, range opening and
container probing together. Expiry returns 504 with Retry-After, cancels pending
work and allows a later retry. A raw response body may outlive startup.

Concurrent store creation and producer turnover share one registered store.
Construction is reserved before releasing the missing-store check, pending
creators keep the rendezvous alive, and selecting a store and registering a
producer lease happen atomically. Factory work and disk cleanup stay outside
registry locks.

A sixth offline fuzz target covers tracker response parsing with bounded,
synthetic inputs. Deterministic tests exercise cancellation, retries, producer
leases and shared construction. Shared CI fuzz and security references advance
together to the reviewed toolkit revision.

This release does not change listener, bind, seeding or cache-path defaults.
It does not add multi-file VOD or claim acceptance against a physical device or
live swarm. Stable and container `latest` channels remain on their prior release.
